# SANRI Foundation Baseline (SFB) v1.0

**Status:** Established  
**Type:** Operational reference milestone  
**Date:** 2026-07-18  
**Owner:** SANRI Product Maturation Program  
**Governance:** SCA v1.0, Accepted & Frozen  

## Era transition

**SANRI Foundation Era:** Completed  
**SANRI Product Era:** Started

SFB v1.0, Foundation Era’nın kapanış ve Product Era’nın başlangıç
milestone’udur. Bu zihinsel geçiş yeni bir anayasal katman oluşturmaz; başarı
ölçütünü tasarım ve belge üretiminden gerçek kullanıcılar için güvenilir,
kaliteli ve sürdürülebilir bir SANRI OS işletmeye taşır.

## Baseline declaration

SFB v1.0 records the point at which SANRI’s initial architecture, unification
and governance foundation is complete:

- Architecture Program — Completed
- Unification Program — Completed
- Governance Program — Completed
- Product Maturation Program — Started
- SCA v1.0 — Accepted & Frozen

From this point forward, SANRI product development is managed through the
Product Maturation Program (PMP). The baseline is a reference label and
release milestone; it is not a constitutional document and does not create a
new governance authority. SFB v1.0 is the single concise reference for the
foundation on which SANRI OS 1.0 is being matured.

## What this baseline means

SFB v1.0 confirms that SANRI has established:

- a domain and application architecture foundation,
- a unified AURA and V1 direction,
- canonical identity and legacy-containment decisions,
- an operational release and evidence process,
- a frozen constitutional governance reference.

The Foundation Era is considered operationally complete as a program. Its
lasting outcome is not document volume; it is the engineering culture of
protecting user trust when schedule, rollout or migration pressure conflicts
with safety.

The Product Era’s single north KPI is **Measured User Value (MUV)**:
important work must create a measurable improvement in real user behavior or
experience. Technical completion without user-value evidence is not product
completion.

It does not claim that migration, production traffic, Alpha, Beta, or SANRI OS
1.0 release gates have been completed. Those are PMP execution outcomes and
must be proven through tests, reports, runbooks and REP evidence.

## Downstream rule

All new product work must:

1. identify its PMP workstream;
2. preserve SCA, SDS, SLS and Release Constitution constraints;
3. add or update evidence appropriate to its release impact;
4. record unresolved risks and rollback impact;
5. avoid creating a parallel constitutional or architectural authority.

## Product Era success checkpoints

The next maturity evidence is operational, not architectural:

- first safe migration,
- first Alpha cohort,
- first release approved with a complete REP,
- first rollback rehearsal or real rollback when required,
- first production incident handled through the defined governance process,
- first SANRI OS 1.0 release.

These checkpoints demonstrate that SCA is being used in practice. They are not
claims that the checkpoints have already been completed.

The next operating focus after SFB v1.0 is:

1. PMP-01 Secure Migration;
2. PMP-02 Alpha Cohort;
3. PMP-03 Context Engine & Project Engine;
4. PMP-04 AI Quality Gate;
5. PMP-05 Beta Release.

> Foundation Era completed. From this point forward, SANRI is expected to
> evolve primarily through disciplined execution rather than expanding its
> constitutional framework.

Türkçesiyle: Foundation Era tamamlanmıştır. Bu noktadan sonra SANRI’nın
gelişiminin temel yolu anayasal çerçeveyi genişletmek değil, tanımlanmış
ilkeleri disiplinli biçimde uygulamaktır.

> Foundation Era has fulfilled its purpose. The quality of SANRI will no
> longer be measured by the sophistication of its governance documents, but by
> the discipline with which they are applied to real users, real releases, and
> real operational decisions.

## Frozen-governance guardrails

Because SCA v1.0 is Accepted & Frozen:

- yeni anayasal belge yalnızca istisnai L4 Constitutional Decision ile
  değerlendirilebilir;
- her yeni fikir otomatik olarak governance seviyesine taşınmaz;
- operasyonel kararlar gereksiz biçimde L4’e yükseltilmez;
- günlük ürün ve engineering kararları SDS, ADR, PMP, runbook veya REP
  kapsamındaki doğru seviyede tutulur.

Bu guardrail’ler SCA’yı büyütmeden ürün hızını ve kararların izlenebilirliğini
korumak için kullanılır.
